Punctuation for Effect in Dialogue

Getting it technically right, then using it to show character

1 / 12

Warm-up

Same words, different feeling

  • "Stop."
  • "Stop!"
  • "Stop..."

2 / 12

The four rules

Punctuating dialogue correctly

  • New speaker → new line
  • Speech goes inside " "
  • First word of the speech gets a capital letter
  • The comma / full stop stays inside the closing mark
3 / 12

The one to drill

Where does the comma go?

✅ "I already told you," he said.
❌ "I already told you", he said.

The comma lives inside the house (the quotation marks) with the words that were spoken.

4 / 12

Beyond the rules

Punctuation as characterisation

  • Short sentences → firm, blunt, in control
  • Questions → curious, unsure, challenging
  • A dash — like this — → interruption, an aside
  • An ellipsis... → hesitation, trailing off

The dash, and the question
